01

Split the original Q sequence into video frames

Before any padding, these are all real tokens. Each complete video frame is 45 rows × 80 tokens per row = 3,600 tokens.

Frame 9 is a partial frame: 896 = 11 complete rows × 80 + 16 additional tokens in row 12. The first eight frames are full.

Why not stop at row 12?

Tile row #2 covers all rows 9–16 because each tile's height is 8. Even if rows 13–16 contain no real Q tokens, their slots belong to the allocated tiles.

The 384 padded slots are 48 coral cells × 8 slots. Those coral cells are what the extra tile rows and row-12 remainder introduce.

Why 500 rather than 464 blocks? Simply rounding 29,696 up to a multiple of 64 would give 464 blocks and no additional token slots. But this benchmark first pads spatial 8 × 8 patches inside each frame, making 480 blocks for frames 1–8 and 20 blocks for frame 9. This is a layout choice, not a mathematical requirement of attention.
