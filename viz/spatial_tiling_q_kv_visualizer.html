What does block_sizes look like for this K/V layout?

420 full blocks have block_sizes[b] = 64. 80 edge blocks have block_sizes[b] = 40. Those 80 short blocks all belong to the first eight complete 45-row frames; all 20 blocks in frame 9 are full. In the cuDNN BSA contract, the value describes valid KV positions, so padded KV keys can be masked.

Why 500 KV blocks? A simple 1D 64-token alignment of 30,080 would yield 470 blocks with zero padding. This benchmark instead first pads spatial 8 × 8 patches within each frame: 8 × 60 = 480 blocks plus 20 from frame 9. The choice of spatial layout introduces 1,920 padding slots.
